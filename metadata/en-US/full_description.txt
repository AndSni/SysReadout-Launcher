SysReadout Launcher is a minimal home screen for people who like to know what their phone is doing. Behind a short list of your apps runs a live, terminal-style readout of the system: pinned status rows, monitor tables and a scrolling event log, styled like an old CRT terminal if you want it to be.

The launcher:
* A handful of pinned apps as clean text entries, limited only by screen height; everything else is one swipe away in a searchable drawer.
* Gestures: swipe up for apps, down for notifications, left/right for an app of your choice, double-tap to lock, long-press for settings.
* Hidden apps, renaming, work profiles.

The readout:
* About 50 optional rows: CPU and memory, battery current and watts, thermal headroom, network speed, Wi-Fi and mobile signal (spelled out, no icons), IP, storage, build properties, GPU, sensors, sunrise, moon phase and more. Pick and order them yourself.
* An editable banner header with live values like {year} and {device}.
* An event stream: network and power changes, installs, app switches and background services.
* Two layouts: classic sections, or a feed where new lines push older ones off the screen, so any amount of data fits.

Deeper monitoring, when you allow it:
* With Shizuku: processes by CPU or memory, every connection per app with server names, wakelocks, per-app battery drain, temperatures, per-core load and system errors.
* With usage access: screen time, unlocks, traffic per app and data used this month.
* With the optional DNS monitor (a local VPN that only carries DNS): which app looks up which server.
* With location, phone, Bluetooth or activity permission: GPS and satellites, cell tower and 5G details, connected Bluetooth devices and their battery, steps.
Every permission is asked only when you switch on the row that needs it.

Make it yours:
* 7 presets including green phosphor and amber DOS terminals, your own saved presets.
* 11 bundled fonts plus your own .ttf/.otf, with size, weight, spacing, case and colour per element.
* CRT effects: glow, scanlines, curvature, vignette, colour fringe, flicker and grain.
* Lock screen: leave it alone, use your own image, or show a snapshot of the log.

The log only runs while the home screen is visible. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere: no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
